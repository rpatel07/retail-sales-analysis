# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance across categories, regions, customer segments, and products.  
The goal is to extract meaningful insights that can help improve decision-making in sales, marketing, and operations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit trends across categories and sub-categories
- Understand customer segment performance
- Identify best-performing regions
- Study impact of discounts on profit
- Analyze time-based sales trends
- Identify top revenue-generating products

---

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset includes retail transaction data with the following features:
- Order details (Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode)
- Customer information (Segment, Region, City)
- Product details (Category, Sub-Category, Product Name)
- Financial metrics (Sales, Profit, Discount)

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Category Analysis
- Compared sales and profit across product categories

### 2. Segment Analysis
- Identified which customer segment contributes most to revenue

### 3. Region Analysis
- Evaluated performance across different regions

### 4. Sub-Category Analysis
- Found top-performing product sub-categories

### 5. Time Series Analysis
- Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends
- Identified seasonality patterns

### 6. Product Analysis
- Identified top revenue-generating products

### 7. Discount vs Profit Analysis
- Studied relationship between discount and profitability

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- Technology category generated the highest sales and profit
- Consumer segment contributed the most revenue
- West region performed best overall
- High discounts negatively impact profit
- Sales and profit show seasonality with peaks in December
- Copiers and printers are top revenue-generating products
- Revenue is concentrated in a small number of high-value products

---

## 📈 Key Visualizations
The project includes:
- Bar charts for category, region, and product analysis
- Line chart for monthly sales/profit trends
- Scatter plot for discount vs profit relationship
